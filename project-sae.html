PROJECT_ID: SAE_AERO_01

Project Objective

The objective of this project was to design and build a micro-class unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) capable of taking off within 9 feet while adhering to strict competition constraints, including a maximum wingspan of 36 inches and a power limit of 450 watts. The aircraft was required to reliably carry a standardized payload while maintaining stable and efficient flight performance.

Technical Description

The aircraft is a high-wing, electric-powered monoplane optimized for short take-off performance. It incorporates a high-camber airfoil, a conventional tail configuration, and an efficient propulsion system selected to meet thrust and power limitations. The overall configuration emphasizes aerodynamic efficiency, stability, and manufacturability while operating within tight size and energy constraints.

Outcomes & Leadership Contributions

  • Captained an 18-member multidisciplinary team, overseeing design, fabrication, testing, and competition readiness.

  • Project was selected at the international level and invited to compete at the SAE Aero Design Series (Micro Class), Lakeland, Florida, USA.

  • Successfully met all performance and regulatory requirements defined by the competition.

  • Achieved an overall 6th place finish globally and 2nd place ranking in Asia, demonstrating strong technical execution and team coordination.
